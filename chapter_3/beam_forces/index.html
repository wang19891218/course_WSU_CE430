Beam Forces from Loads and Support Motion

Apply a load, rotate or settle a support — and watch the forces that develop in the member, live.

This is a live physics simulation (not a canned animation): a beam is discretized into many small segments, each with mass, damping, and bending stiffness, and integrated forward in time. Push on it with a load, or move one of its supports, and watch it deform, overshoot a little, and settle — while the end moments and support reactions it develops are drawn on the beam with their live magnitudes, and the bending-moment diagram updates underneath.
