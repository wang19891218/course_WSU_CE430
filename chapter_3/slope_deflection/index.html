Beam Deflection & Curvature — Interactive

Drag a load or move a support and watch a real, dynamically-integrated beam settle into its new shape.

This is a live physics simulation (not a canned animation): a beam is discretized into many small segments, each with mass, damping, and bending stiffness, and integrated forward in time. Apply a load, rotate or settle a support, and watch the beam actually respond — deform, overshoot a little, and settle — the same way a real member would. The slope-deflection method you're covering in class works with exactly these ingredients: end rotations, support settlement, and the moments they produce.
